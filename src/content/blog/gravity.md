# The Nature of Gravity

Gravity remains the most elusive of the fundamental forces. While the Standard Model beautifully describes electromagnetism, the weak force, and the strong force, gravity stubbornly resists quantization.

## String Theory

In string theory, gravity emerges naturally as a closed string excitation, specifically a spin-2 massless state that we identify with the graviton.

$$ G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu} $$
